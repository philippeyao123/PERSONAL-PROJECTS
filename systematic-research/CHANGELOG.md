# Changelog

All notable changes are documented in this file. The format follows Keep a Changelog and versions
follow Semantic Versioning.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-27

### Added

- point-in-time data schema and historical investment universe;
- momentum, carry, and value features, transformations, and neutralization;
- portfolio construction, constraints, and volatility targeting;
- vectorized backtesting, linear costs, square-root impact, and capacity analysis;
- risk, performance, attribution, PSR/DSR, IC/IR, and stability diagnostics;
- rolling or expanding walk-forward validation with purging, embargo, and nested selection;
- deterministic tracking, cache, exports, reporting, and CLI;
- TSMOM, statistical-arbitrage, and flagship examples;
- unit, anti-leakage, and integration tests, typing, linting, CI, and distributions.
