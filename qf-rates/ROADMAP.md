# Roadmap

## v0.1.0 — complete in this source tree

Curves, swaps, analytic options, G2++ bonds and swaptions, calibration, Monte-Carlo validation,
Bermudan LSM, DV01/vega/scenarios, tests, CI, packaging and documentation.

## v0.2.0

- Date/calendar/day-count types and market-standard schedule generation.
- Multi-curve bootstrap with deposits, FRAs, futures, OIS and swaps.
- Exact conditional one-dimensional G2++ swaption integration as a second engine.
- Sobol sequences, Brownian bridge, common random numbers and adjoint/pathwise Greeks.
- Collateral/CSA mechanics, stochastic credit intensities and exposure regression.
- QuantLib-backed golden dataset generated in CI.

## v0.3.0

- Callable structures, caps/floors, cross-currency swaps and multi-currency XVA.
- Production-grade SIMM sensitivities and official risk-weight configuration.
- Stable Python wheels and portfolio-level parallelism.
